## Risks to carry, not the headline

- eBay Live appears in this collection mostly as promotion: 22 direct-pull TikTok captions name it (13 paid partnerships from 12 creators; 8 seller or host announcements or a pricing-tool ad; 1 hashtag-only mention, author role unknown), 0 hobby-pull captions, 13 YouTube titles (3 from eBay's own channels), 16 Reddit and YouTube comments. First-person experiences are few: three purchase or order accounts (mid-auction box switch; a card won after a queue and 15-second auctions, with a helpful host; watches delivered but on-stream trade-in promises unhonoured), one coin-stream observer, one unsuccessful bidder. This cannot establish overall buyer sentiment; no unpaid positive first-person buyer account was established in the reviewed evidence.
- Whatnot buyers describe the same frictions about Whatnot (sudden-death auctions of 3 to 5 seconds, swipe-bid jumps, notification pressure, hype buying). Live-format friction is not eBay-specific.
- Authentication is the shared trust topic and cuts both ways in cards, sneakers, watches and handbags: creases caught and fakes refused sit in the same threads as AG-passed damaged cards, an authenticator's mistake and "seller protection is basically a myth". The bullion rows read do not mention AG; coverage was not verified.
- Absent from these pulls: UK Panini stickers and Premier League (0 rows), match-worn (0), Fanatics Live (0), Riftbound (0), German-stated sneaker or card buyers (0). Absence here is a pull fact.

## What the community evidence says to each business-side input (Community Opportunity × Business Opportunity × Right to Play)

This collection covers the community side only and cannot rank communities. Business inputs should decide scale; the table above is a conditional action menu, not seven promises.
- Live GMV and buyer dynamics by category: unknown here. If cards and TCG carry Live, the shoppers who talk are comp- and condition-checkers, and the visible frictions are auction speed, mid-auction changes and AG handling.
- Supply: sellers judge eBay Live by fees, viewers, "clientele" and store traffic; several r/Ebay commenters call it force-fed; one comics business says it sells well at fair market value. Seller opinion, not buyer data.
- Competitive position: Whatnot is described by its own sellers as a liquidation venue and by its buyers with the same complaints made about eBay Live; TikTok Shop is where the hobby pull's live promotion goes (breaks, clone consoles, preloved bags, patches).
- Right to play: authentication is eBay's visible differentiator in cards, sneakers, watches and luxury, contested in each; AG logistics (hub delays) are a named friction.
- Sneakers: the call deprioritised them for Live; the sprint Q&A lists them as right-to-win. Reported, not resolved.
- Coins and P&A: no community evidence either way for Live; discovery questions.
- Handles: the client's direction is @eBay and @eBayLive first; proposals above name a route each (seller or creator handle with a core-handle clip, or @eBayLive-led where an authenticator is the draw). Nothing in the community evidence argues for a dedicated handle now.

## Missing business inputs

These establish performance; the social accounts above already give self-reported experiences.
